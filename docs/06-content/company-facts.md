## 5. QCA registration — the most valuable underused asset

The legacy site states, in body copy on the homepage only, that Vedanjay Power is a **registered
QCA (Qualified Coordinating Agency) in Maharashtra, Telangana and Madhya Pradesh**.

Why this matters: QCA registration is a *regulator-granted status*, not a marketing claim. Under
state Forecasting, Scheduling & Deviation Settlement regulations, only a registered QCA may
aggregate and schedule renewable generators at the state load despatch centre. It is a licence to
operate in the highest-margin service Vedanjay offers, it is externally checkable against SLDC
registers, and very few firms hold it in three states.

> block — not buried in a paragraph. See `docs/02-information-architecture/page-specifications.md`.
